Choosing a Studio

How to Choose the Best Tattoo Shop in Mumbai

Mumbai has hundreds of tattoo studios, and "best" means different things to different people. Instead of asking which studio is universally best, here's what actually separates a good tattoo experience from a regrettable one — and how Vision Tattoo Shop approaches each one.

01

Artist Skill & Style Match

Not every artist is good at every style. Before choosing a studio, look at real portfolio work in the specific style you want — realism, fine line, geometric, script, or cover-up — rather than a generic "we do everything" claim. At Vision Tattoo Shop, our artists specialise across different styles, and you're matched with the artist best suited to your design during your consultation.

02

Hygiene & Sterilisation Standards

Tattooing breaks the skin, so hygiene isn't optional — it's the baseline. A trustworthy studio uses single-use, sealed needles opened in front of you, medical-grade disinfectants, and barrier-protected workstations that are reset between every client.

03

Consultation & Design Process

A good tattoo starts long before the machine turns on. We spend time understanding your idea, placement, sizing, and how the design will age on your skin — before any drawing begins.

04

Transparent Pricing

No vague quotes, no last-minute add-ons. You should know your price before your appointment, not after.

05

Reviews & Real Client Experience

Look for consistent, verifiable reviews (Google, not just social media) rather than one-off praise.

Hygiene & Safety

Hygiene & Safety at Vision Tattoo Shop

Tattooing is a skin procedure, and we treat it that way. Every session at Vision Tattoo Shop follows a strict hygiene protocol:

  • Single-use, disposable needles — opened in front of you
  • Sealed, branded inks from reputed manufacturers
  • Fresh gloves for every client, changed as needed
  • Medical-grade disinfectants and surface cleaning
  • Barrier protection on machines, cables, and workstations
  • Proper disposal systems for needles and contaminated waste
  • Consent forms and allergy/medical questions before every session
